# IT-Expenditure-Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **IT Expenditure Analysis** project focuses on analyzing IT spending across different business areas, regions, departments, and cost categories using Power BI.

The objective of this analysis is to provide transparency into actual, forecast, and planned IT expenditure, identify budget variances, and uncover opportunities for cost optimization.

This analysis helps organizations monitor spending patterns, evaluate budget performance, and support strategic financial decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze IT expenditure across different business areas and regions.
- Compare Actual, Forecast, and Planned IT spending.
- Identify budget variances and spending discrepancies.
- Analyze cost drivers across IT areas and cost categories.
- Identify departments and regions with significant deviations from budget.
- Support cost optimization and strategic budgeting decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset handling and data preparation.
- **Data Analytics** – Exploratory data analysis and variance analysis.

---

## 📂 Project Analysis

### 1. Data Preparation
- Loaded and explored the dataset.
- Standardized column names for consistency.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Formatted dates and categorical fields.
- Organized the data for analysis and visualization.

### 2. Expenditure Analysis
- Analyzed total IT expenditure across different categories.
- Compared Actual, Forecast, and Planned spending.
- Evaluated spending trends across time periods.
- Identified differences between actual spending and budgeted expenditure.

### 3. Departmental & Regional Analysis
- Segmented expenditure by IT Area, Business Area, Region, and Cost Element Group.
- Compared spending patterns across departments and regions.
- Identified areas with potential budget deviations.
- Examined expenditure patterns across internal labor and shared services.

### 4. Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard analysis focuses on:

- IT expenditure overview.
- Actual vs. Plan vs. Forecast comparison.
- Budget variance analysis.
- Regional and departmental expenditure.
- Cost distribution across IT areas.
- Spending trends and potential cost drivers.
